Adobe + ChatGPT Persona Behaviors

Behavior-only showcase for 10 synthetic testing personas. Each card lists multiple observable behaviors for isolated integration testing.

P001

Maya Singh

  1. PromptingP001-B01

    Starts with a short directive and expects ChatGPT to ask only one clarifying question if needed.

  2. WorkflowP001-B02

    Requests multiple social-ready variations before choosing a final creative direction.

  3. ReviewP001-B03

    Checks whether generated copy is energetic but does not exaggerate campaign claims.

  4. PrivacyP001-B04

    Removes confidential launch names from prompts and uses placeholder campaign labels.

  5. AccessibilityP001-B05

    Asks for contrast-friendly text placement and concise alt-text guidance for exported assets.

P002

Oliver Grant

  1. PromptingP002-B01

    Writes numbered instructions that define brand tone, forbidden wording, and approval gates.

  2. WorkflowP002-B02

    Asks ChatGPT to separate creative exploration from final production guidance.

  3. ReviewP002-B03

    Inspects every output for brand drift, off-brand colors, and unsupported claims.

  4. PrivacyP002-B04

    Uses sanitized campaign briefs and requests reminders before sharing sensitive files.

  5. AccessibilityP002-B05

    Requests readable summaries and keyboard-accessible steps for sign-off documentation.

P003

Sofia Martinez

  1. PromptingP003-B01

    Describes composition, texture, emotion, and color mood before asking for edits.

  2. WorkflowP003-B02

    Iterates slowly, changing one visual variable at a time to preserve creative intent.

  3. ReviewP003-B03

    Compares outputs against her sketch notes and rejects results that feel generic.

  4. PrivacyP003-B04

    Asks whether uploaded artwork remains private and requests non-training wording in test prompts.

  5. AccessibilityP003-B05

    Requests larger previews and simple Spanish-English explanations for complex edit steps.

P004

Daniel Chen

  1. PromptingP004-B01

    Frames requests as training scenarios with learner goals, audience level, and success rubric.

  2. WorkflowP004-B02

    Breaks creative tasks into repeatable steps that can be documented for teammates.

  3. ReviewP004-B03

    Checks whether the result teaches a clear concept without visual clutter.

  4. PrivacyP004-B04

    Replaces real department names, employee examples, and internal metrics with neutral samples.

  5. AccessibilityP004-B05

    Requests captions, accessible reading order, and simple instructions for screen-reader users.

P005

Aisha Okafor

  1. PromptingP005-B01

    Explains the mission first and asks ChatGPT to keep language respectful, hopeful, and direct.

  2. WorkflowP005-B02

    Requests a low-effort workflow that volunteers can repeat without advanced design skills.

  3. ReviewP005-B03

    Reviews outputs for dignity, cultural sensitivity, and donor-safe wording.

  4. PrivacyP005-B04

    Removes beneficiary names, faces, locations, and donor details unless explicit consent is confirmed.

  5. AccessibilityP005-B05

    Asks for large readable text, mobile previews, and simple calls to action.

P006

Ethan Brooks

  1. PromptingP006-B01

  2. WorkflowP006-B02

    Rapidly tests bold alternatives and asks for quick ranking of the strongest option.

  3. ReviewP006-B03

    Checks whether the result looks fresh, shareable, and not like a template.

  4. PrivacyP006-B04

    Needs prompts to remind him not to include student IDs, private chats, or location details.

  5. AccessibilityP006-B05

    Requests captions and readable text overlays because most classmates view content on mobile.

  2. WorkflowP007-B02

  3. ReviewP007-B03

  4. PrivacyP007-B04

P008

Lucas Meyer

  1. PromptingP008-B01

    Writes prompts like QA cases with preconditions, steps, expected result, and failure signals.

  2. WorkflowP008-B02

    Requests exact checkpoints for keyboard use, reading order, contrast, and text alternatives.

  3. ReviewP008-B03

    Challenges vague AI recommendations and asks for measurable accessibility evidence.

  4. PrivacyP008-B04

    Uses anonymized test cases instead of real disability disclosures or user research quotes.

  5. AccessibilityP008-B05

    Requires every creative output to include alt-text notes, contrast checks, and screen-reader assumptions.

P009

Hana Kim

  1. PromptingP009-B01

    Begins with compliance limits and asks ChatGPT to flag anything requiring legal review.

  2. WorkflowP009-B02

    Requests conservative edits and rejects broad creative rewrites that change approved meaning.

  3. ReviewP009-B03

    Checks disclaimers, regulated wording, consent language, and recordkeeping notes before approval.

  4. PrivacyP009-B04

    Never uploads contracts, names, personal identifiers, or privileged legal communications.

  5. AccessibilityP009-B05

    Asks for plain-language summaries that separate creative suggestions from legal-risk notes.

  2. WorkflowP010-B02

  3. ReviewP010-B03

    Evaluates whether the final asset looks professional enough for local customers.

  4. PrivacyP010-B04
