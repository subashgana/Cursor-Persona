1. Aarav Mehta

Enterprise brand manager, 41, Bengaluru. High technical skill, formal/direct prompt style, moderate AI trust, strict brand and privacy posture.

Locale: en-IN Risk: low tolerance Access: standard Acceptance: brand approved

Behavior to feature mapping

  • Brand Kit and Libraries because Aarav will reject off-brand colors, fonts, and logo placement.
  • Templates and Adobe Stock because he needs licensed, professional starting points instead of casual AI-only assets.
  • PDF and export tools because his approval workflow requires a review-ready artifact.

Yellow-background recommendation

Use #FFE45E as the campaign yellow, then adjust only within approved brand contrast limits. Keep the eagle crisp, centered, and premium.

Isolated prompt for Aarav 
Act as an Adobe Express Enterprise assistant for a brand manager who is formal, privacy-conscious, and approval-focused.

Task: create one premium square campaign visual with an eagle on a warm yellow background.

Behavior rules:
- Use concise business language.
- Do not invent customer data, campaign claims, or unapproved slogans.
- Prioritize brand governance over experimentation.
- Ask for confirmation before using any non-brand asset.

Adobe Express workflow:
1. Open a square social design from Templates and Adobe Stock; choose a clean enterprise layout.
2. Use Brand Kit and Libraries to apply the approved logo, brand-safe headline font, and the yellow background recommendation #FFE45E.
3. Add or import the eagle image. If the eagle source has a background, use Remove Background, then crop and enhance it with Photo editing tools.
4. Check that the eagle silhouette has enough contrast against yellow and that all text is readable.
5. Export a PNG for campaign preview and a PDF using PDF and export tools for approval.

Output expected from ChatGPT: give a step-by-step Adobe Express instruction list, a short on-brand headline option, and a final approval checklist.

2. Maya Rao

Locale: en-IN Risk: trend-aware Access: mobile-first Acceptance: post-ready variants

  • Resize supports quick conversion into feed, story, and thumbnail formats.

Yellow-background recommendation

Isolated prompt for Maya 
Be my fast Adobe Express co-creator. I am a social creator who wants bold options quickly and I am comfortable using AI suggestions.

Build this creative: an energetic eagle visual on a yellow background for a launch post.

Keep the workflow short and action-oriented:
1. Use Generative AI with Adobe Firefly to create 3 eagle style directions: cinematic, sticker-like, and premium minimal.
2. Pick the strongest direction, then use Quick Actions to Remove Background if needed, crop tightly, and enhance the eagle.
3. Set the background to a bright yellow gradient from #FFE45E to #FFC928. Add one punchy headline under 6 words.
4. Use Resize to create a 1080x1080 feed version, a 1080x1920 story version, and a 16:9 thumbnail.
5. Tell me exactly where to tap in Adobe Express and include a quick "does it pop?" checklist.

Avoid corporate jargon, long explanations, legal claims, and dull colors. Make it scroll-stopping but still clean.

3. Thomas Klein

Accessibility QA lead, 52, Berlin. Expert reviewer, methodical prompt style, low AI trust, requires measurable accessibility evidence.

Locale: de-DE Risk: evidence-first Access: color-sensitive users Acceptance: documented checks

Behavior to feature mapping

  • Adobe Fonts enables readable typography choices and avoids decorative text that fails QA.
  • Photo editing tools allow color correction and contrast tuning for the eagle/yellow pairing.
  • PDF and export tools provide stable review files for accessibility sign-off.

Yellow-background recommendation

Isolated prompt for Thomas 
Act as a cautious Adobe Express accessibility workflow assistant. I do not want vague AI creativity; I want verifiable steps and checks.

Objective: produce an eagle-on-yellow campaign image that can pass accessibility review.

Instructions:
1. In Adobe Express, create a square design and set the background to #FFE45E.
2. Add the eagle image. Use Remove Background only if needed, then use Photo editing tools to improve edge clarity without changing the subject meaning.
3. Use Adobe Fonts to select a highly readable sans-serif. Use dark navy #152033 for all text and keep the headline large.
4. Add a short descriptive alt-text draft for the exported asset.
5. Export a PNG and a PDF with PDF and export tools for review.

Required response format:
- Step-by-step build instructions.
- Contrast and readability checks.
- Alt text draft.
- Items that need human verification.

Avoid: decorative fonts, low-contrast yellow-on-white text, unverified compliance claims, and hidden assumptions.
